WhatsApp Link Generator — Complete Guide

A WhatsApp click-to-chat link (wa.me link) lets anyone start a conversation with you on WhatsApp with one click — no need to save your number first. This guide explains how to create one, where to use it, and how to write effective pre-filled messages.

What is a wa.me Link?

A wa.me link is a standard URL in the format https://wa.me/PHONE_NUMBER. When someone clicks it, WhatsApp opens automatically with a new chat to that number. With an optional pre-filled message, it also inserts text into the message box so the user just has to press Send.

Step-by-Step: Creating Your WhatsApp Link

Step 1 — Enter your phone numberEnter the number in international format — country code + number, no special characters. For India: 919876543210 (91 = India code, then 10 digits). Do not include +, spaces, or dashes.
Step 2 — Write a pre-filled message (optional but recommended)Add a default message that pre-populates when the customer opens the chat. Example for a service business: Hi, I saw your post on Instagram and would like to know more about your services.
Step 3 — Generate and copy the linkClick Generate. Copy the wa.me URL that appears. You can also scan the QR code for physical use on business cards or flyers.
Step 4 — Add it to your platformsPaste the link in your Instagram bio, website Contact page, email signature, or LinkedIn. The more places it appears, the more leads you get.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the person need to save my number to use the wa.me link?
No. That's the main advantage of a wa.me link. The person clicks it and can chat with you without saving your number first.
Can I use this for a WhatsApp Business number?
Yes. wa.me links work identically for personal and WhatsApp Business numbers.
What happens if WhatsApp is not installed?
The wa.me link opens the WhatsApp Web version in a browser if the app is not installed.
Can I include emojis in the pre-filled message?
Yes. The generator handles emoji encoding automatically.
